GiveLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

In shortGiveLedger has no account, no ads and no tracking. The gifts and receipts you add stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell or share them.

What you enter

GiveLedger works with what you add: charitable gifts (charity name, date, amount, type, items and their condition and value, an optional note, and which records you have). This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. We don't have one.

Receipts, camera and photos

You can attach a receipt by scanning it with the camera, choosing a photo, or picking a file. GiveLedger uses the camera only while you scan, and the photo picker only shows the photos you choose. The receipt is saved inside the app on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Backups and reports

You can save a backup file or share a PDF or spreadsheet report. You choose where these go (for example Files, iCloud Drive, email or AirDrop). Your app data can also be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, if you have those turned on. Those are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Purchases

GiveLedger Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we receive no card or personal details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code in the app
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't collect any

Children

GiveLedger is a record-keeping tool for adults and isn't directed at children.

Not tax advice

GiveLedger shows the IRS record-keeping rules for charitable gifts. It doesn't decide what you can deduct and isn't tax advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: support@parthdarji.com.
