GiveLedger

Questions

What records does the IRS ask for?

For any cash gift, a bank or card record, or a written record from the charity. For a single gift of $250 or more, a written acknowledgment from the charity, which you should have before you file. For donated goods, a receipt from the charity or your own written list of what you gave, where and when. GiveLedger flags a gift when one of these is missing.

When do I need Form 8283?

When the total you claim for donated goods (noncash gifts) for the year is more than $500. GiveLedger adds up your goods and tells you when you pass that line.

How should I value used clothes or furniture?

At fair market value: roughly what a buyer would pay for the item used, such as a thrift-shop price, not what you paid new. Clothing and household items generally need to be in good used condition or better.

Does GiveLedger file my taxes or tell me what I can deduct?

No. It keeps your records organized and shows the IRS record-keeping rules. Whether and how much you can deduct depends on your situation; ask a tax professional.
