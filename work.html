How I work

  1. You send a short brief by email: the problem, who it's for, must-have features.
  2. I reply with a written plan: scope, milestones, a fixed price or estimate, and timeline.
  3. Weekly written updates with TestFlight builds or preview links you can try.
  4. Launch and handover: App Store submission, source code in your repository, and notes for the next developer.

Principles

  • Native and fast: no heavy frameworks when the platform already does the job.
  • Private by default: data stays on the device unless the product truly needs a server.
  • Small and focused: one problem solved well beats ten half-done features.
