TurnBright

Stop scheduling Airbnb cleans by text message.

TurnBright is being built for short-term rental cleaning companies with 2–20 cleaners. It reads your hosts' Airbnb and VRBO calendars, schedules every turnover automatically, and sends the host a photo report when the clean is done.

Get early access

Free during early access. No calls needed: everything happens by email.

Auto-scheduled turnovers

Connect a host's calendar link once. Every checkout becomes a clean on your schedule, and changed bookings update it.

Photo checklists for cleaners

Each cleaner sees their jobs on their phone with a room-by-room checklist and required photos.

Proof-of-clean reports

Hosts get a link with photos the moment a clean is finished. Fewer "was it cleaned?" messages.

Damage and supply notes

Cleaners flag broken items or low supplies with a photo, and the host is told right away.

How it works

  1. Add your hosts' properties and paste each calendar link.
  2. Assign cleaners, or let TurnBright suggest who is free.
  3. Cleaners complete the checklist on their phone.
  4. The host receives the photo report automatically.
