Ownary Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

Welcome to Ownary: Home Inventory Vault. We built Ownary to give homeowners, renters, and collectors a secure, private way to catalog home valuables, receipts, serial numbers, and warranties without cloud privacy risks or recurring subscriptions. Because your home asset data is deeply personal, Ownary is engineered with a strict 100% on-device, zero-telemetry architecture.

🔒 Zero-Cloud Guarantee: Ownary does not collect, transmit, store, or monetize your inventory items, photos, receipts, serial numbers, or insurance dossiers. All data is stored 100% locally on your Apple device using encrypted SwiftData storage.

1. Home Inventory & Receipt Data

All items, photos, purchase receipts, serial numbers, warranty dates, and maintenance logs you enter are stored strictly within your device's local application sandbox:

  • Local SwiftData Storage: Your records are saved directly on your iPhone or iPad. No data is ever transmitted to any third-party cloud server.
  • Biometric Security (Face ID / Touch ID): If enabled, your vault is protected by Apple's secure LocalAuthentication biometric framework. Your biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave.
  • Export Control: When you generate an Insurance Claim PDF dossier or CSV spreadsheet, the file is created on-device and shared only via iOS system share sheet at your explicit direction.

2. Camera & Vision Scanner

Ownary requests camera access solely for on-device barcode scanning, serial number recognition, and document scanning (powered by Apple's native VisionKit). All OCR text extraction occurs directly on your device's Neural Engine. No camera feeds or photos are ever uploaded or transmitted externally.

3. Reminders & Calendar Integration

If you choose to sync warranty expiration or return window deadlines, Ownary utilizes Apple's native EventKit and UserNotifications frameworks. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device without push notification servers.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Ownary Pro is offered as a single one-time lifetime purchase. All transactions are handled securely by Apple via the official StoreKit 2 API. We never handle, store, or have access to your credit card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

5. Zero Third-Party Analytics & Zero Ads

Ownary contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking pixels, and zero telemetry frameworks (no Firebase, no Meta SDK, no Mixpanel). We do not collect identifiers, device fingerprints, or behavioral analytics.
