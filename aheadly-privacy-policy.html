Aheadly Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

In shortAheadly has no account, no bank login, no ads and no tracking. The income and tax payments you enter stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell or share them.

What you enter

Aheadly works with the numbers you type in: payments from clients (amount, client name, date, an optional note), estimated tax payments you record, and your tax settings (filing status, expected income, state rate, last year's tax). This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. We don't have one.

Backups

Like other app data, Aheadly's data can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, if you have those turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders (Pro), Aheadly schedules notifications on your iPhone for the days before each due date. They are created and shown by your device; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Aheadly Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we receive no card or personal details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up
  • No connection to your bank or payment apps
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code in the app
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't collect any

Exports and email

If you export a CSV or send us feedback, you choose where it goes. Feedback emails include your app version, iOS version and whether Pro is active, so we can help. We use them only to reply and fix problems.

Children

Aheadly is a tax tool for adults and isn't directed at children.

Not tax advice

Aheadly gives estimates based on IRS rates. It doesn't file or pay taxes for you and isn't tax advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: support@parthdarji.com.
