Why these apps

Each one fixes a single paperwork job that is easy to forget until it costs money. With an HSA, you can pay yourself back for a medical bill years later, but only if you can still find the receipt. Freelancers and 1099 contractors have to send the IRS estimated tax four times a year, and a missed quarter means an underpayment penalty. Since tax year 2026, people who take the standard deduction can deduct some cash gifts to charity, which makes good donation records matter to many more households.

How they work

All three apps work without an account and keep your records on your iPhone. You choose when to make a backup and where it goes. In ShoeboxHSA and GiveLedger you can scan receipts with the camera or attach them from Files, then share one PDF report with yourself or your tax preparer.

The apps show the official IRS rules and link the sources, so you can check them yourself. They do not file your taxes or decide what you can deduct.
