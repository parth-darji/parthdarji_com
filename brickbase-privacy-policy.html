BrickBase Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BrickBase ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application and BLoC clean architecture dashboard service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide construction operations tracking, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Details: Your name, email address, password, company role, and company name when you sign up.
  • Construction Site Data: Attendance logs, visitor logs, blueprints, inventory, and materials lists uploaded to our Cloudflare R2 databases.
  • Location Data: Geofencing coordinates and GPS stamps used strictly to verify site supervisor attendance locations.
  • Payment Logs: Transaction references from In-App Purchases (App Store and Google Play billing) processed securely via Apple and Google.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following operational tasks:

  • To set up and manage company multi-tenant workspaces.
  • To compile and generate PDF attendance, payroll, and material reports.
  • To sync offline Drift SQLite cache databases securely when internet connectivity is active.
  • To process your subscriptions and payments.

3. Data Sharing and Security

  • Security: All database transactions are encrypted in transit via HTTPS and secured behind industry-standard database access lists.
  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell your personal data or operational spreadsheets to any third-party advertising brokers.
