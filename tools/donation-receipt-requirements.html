Donation receipt requirements

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortEvery money gift needs proof: a bank record or a note from the charity with its name, the date and the amount. For any single gift of $250 or more, you need a written acknowledgment from the charity, and you need it before you file.

Any money gift, any size

Keep one of these:

  • A bank record: canceled check, bank or credit card statement, or electronic transfer receipt.
  • A written communication from the charity (receipt, letter or email) showing its name, the date and the amount.

Money includes cash, checks, card payments, online payments and payroll deductions.

Single gifts of $250 or more

You need a contemporaneous written acknowledgment from the charity. It should include:

  1. The charity's name.
  2. The amount of money given, or a description (not the value) of donated items.
  3. Whether you got any goods or services in return, and if so, a good-faith estimate of their value. If the only thing you got was an intangible religious benefit, it should say so.

Your Social Security number is not needed on it. A canceled check alone is not enough for these gifts.

The deadline

"Contemporaneous" means you have it by the earlier of the day you file your return or the return's due date (with extensions). Ask for missing letters in January, not in April.

Small gifts are not added together

The $250 rule looks at each single gift. Weekly $50 offerings to your church don't need the acknowledgment just because the year adds up to more than $250. You still need a record of each one.

Got something in return? The $75 rule

If you pay more than $75 and get something back (a dinner, a ticket), the charity must give you a statement that you can only deduct the part above the value of what you received, with a good-faith estimate of that value. Small token items and some membership perks don't count.

Donated items

Goods have their own rules for value, condition and Form 8283. See how to value donated items and Form 8283 explained, or build a list with the donation log builder.

Questions

What does a donation receipt need to include?

For any money gift: the charity's name, the date and the amount, or a bank record. For a single gift of $250 or more, a written acknowledgment with the amount (or a description of items) and whether you received anything in return.

When do I need the $250 acknowledgment?

By the earlier of the day you file your tax return or the return's due date including extensions.

Do small gifts that add up to $250 need an acknowledgment?

No. The rule applies to each single gift. Separate gifts under $250 are not added together, but you still need a record of each.
