Questions

How do you calculate the cost of a meeting?

Attendees × average hourly pay (plus overhead) × hours. Six people at $85,000 a year with 30% overhead cost about $319 for a one-hour meeting.

How much do weekly meetings cost a year?

The cost of one meeting times 52. A $319 weekly meeting costs about $16,600 a year.
