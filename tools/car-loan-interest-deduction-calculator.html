Car loan interest deduction calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortFor 2025 through 2028, you can deduct up to $10,000 a year of interest on a loan for a new car, SUV, truck, van or motorcycle with final assembly in the United States, bought for personal use. It shrinks above $100,000 of income ($200,000 joint).

Check final assembly on the window sticker or with the NHTSA VIN decoder. Your lender should report the interest paid. Business-use vehicles, leases and used cars don't qualify. You'll need the VIN on your return. An estimate based on IRS guidance as of November 2026.

Questions

Can I deduct car loan interest in 2026?

Yes, up to $10,000 a year for 2025 through 2028, if the loan is for a new, US-assembled vehicle bought after 2024 for personal use. It phases out above $100,000 of income ($200,000 joint).

Do used cars or leases qualify for the car loan interest deduction?

No. Only new vehicles bought with a loan secured by the vehicle qualify.
