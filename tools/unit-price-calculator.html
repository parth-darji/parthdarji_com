Questions

How do you calculate unit price?

Divide the price by the size in a common unit. $5.99 for 24 oz is $0.25 per ounce; compare that with other sizes or brands in the same unit.

Is buying in bulk always cheaper?

Often but not always; compare unit prices, since sales and store brands can make smaller packs cheaper, and only buy what you'll use.
