1099 forms in January 2027: what freelancers get

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortClients must send your 2026 Form 1099-NEC by February 1, 2027 (January 31 is a Sunday). Payment apps and marketplaces send a 1099-K when your payments are over $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, but you can get one below that.

Form 1099-NEC

A client who paid you for work as a contractor files a 1099-NEC with the IRS and gives you a copy. The legal due date is January 31; when that falls on a weekend, it moves to the next business day.

Form 1099-K

Payment apps and online marketplaces report payments over $20,000 in more than 200 transactions. Some send one anyway. Card payments through a processor can also show up on a 1099-K. See 1099-NEC vs 1099-K.

Report all your income, form or not

You owe tax on self-employment income even if no form arrives. Use your own records and invoices. If the same payment is on a 1099-NEC and a 1099-K, report it once.

Wrong or missing form?

Ask the sender for a corrected form. Keep proof of what you were actually paid.

Don't forget January 15

The last 2026 estimated tax payment is due January 15, 2027, before most forms arrive. See the January 15 payment guide.

Questions

When will I get my 1099-NEC for 2026?

Clients must send it by February 1, 2027, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday.

What is the 1099-K threshold?

Payment apps and online marketplaces report when payments are over $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, though you can receive one below that.

Do I have to report income if I don't get a 1099?

Yes. Self-employment income is taxable whether or not you receive a form.
