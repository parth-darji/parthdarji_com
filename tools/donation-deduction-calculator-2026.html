2026 donation deduction calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortFrom tax year 2026, people who take the standard deduction can deduct cash gifts to qualified charities up to $1,000, or $2,000 if married filing jointly. Enter your gifts to see how much counts.

Cash means check, card, bank transfer or online payment. The charity must be a qualified organization: check it with the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search. This shows the deduction, not your tax savings, which depend on your tax bracket.

Questions

How much can I deduct for charity in 2026 without itemizing?

Up to $1,000 of cash gifts to qualified charities, or $2,000 if married filing jointly.

How do I check if a charity is tax-deductible?

Search for it in the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search at apps.irs.gov/app/eos.

Does this calculator store my numbers?

No. It runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
