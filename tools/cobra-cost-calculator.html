Ways to pay less

  • Compare the marketplace. Losing job coverage lets you enroll at HealthCare.gov within 60 days, and you may qualify for savings based on income.
  • Use the 60-day election window. You have 60 days to elect COBRA, and coverage is retroactive once you pay.
  • Use your HSA. COBRA premiums are one of the few premiums your HSA can pay. See HSA and insurance premiums.

Questions

How much does COBRA cost?

Up to 102% of the full premium: your share plus your employer's share, plus a 2% admin fee. Many people pay three to five times what came out of their paycheck.

How long does COBRA last?

Usually up to 18 months after leaving a job. A disability extension can add 11 months, and some events for family members allow up to 36 months.

Can I pay COBRA with my HSA?

Yes. COBRA premiums are a qualified HSA expense.
