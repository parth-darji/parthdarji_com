Tips that actually help

  • Set the total first, then split it across people. It's easier to stick to one number than to twenty.
  • Don't forget the extras. Food, wrapping and shipping often add 20-40% on top of gifts.
  • Pay cash or debit if you can. Holiday spending on a credit card at 20%+ interest can cost you for months.
  • Agree on limits with family: a gift exchange or a price cap per person saves everyone money.

Questions

How much should I spend on Christmas gifts?

Start from what you can pay without debt, then split it across people. Many people set a per-person cap and budget 20-40% extra for food, wrapping and shipping.

How do I make a holiday budget?

List everyone you buy for with an amount, add extras like food, decorations, travel and shipping, compare the total to what you can afford, and save a set amount each week until the holiday.
