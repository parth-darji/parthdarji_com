1099-NEC vs 1099-K

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In short1099-NEC comes from a client who paid you directly. 1099-K comes from a payment app, card processor or marketplace. Either way, all business income is taxable, even if you get no form at all.

Form 1099-NEC

  • Sent by a business that paid you for work as a non-employee (freelancer, contractor).
  • For payments made in 2026 and later, a client sends one when it paid you $2,000 or more in the year. It was $600 before, and the new amount will be adjusted for inflation from 2027.
  • Usually arrives by January 31.

Form 1099-K

  • Sent by payment apps and online marketplaces (third-party settlement organizations) when they paid you more than $20,000 and more than 200 payments in the year. Some states use lower thresholds, so you may get one anyway.
  • Card processors report card payments with no minimum.
  • It shows the gross amount, before fees, refunds and shipping.

Don't count the same money twice

If a client paid you through a payment app, the same income can appear on a 1099-NEC and a 1099-K. Report your real business income once on Schedule C, and keep records showing how the totals line up.

No form? Still taxable

Below the thresholds you may get no form, but the income still counts. Keep your own list of every payment, with date, client and amount.

What to do with them

  1. Compare each form with your own records.
  2. Report the income on Schedule C, then subtract your business expenses.
  3. Your profit sets your self-employment tax and income tax. See both in the 1099 tax calculator.

Sources

  • IRS Publication 1099: General instructions for information returns
  • IRS: Form 1099-K FAQs

Questions

What is the 1099-NEC threshold for 2026?

For payments made in 2026, a business generally sends a 1099-NEC when it paid you $2,000 or more in the year. It was $600 before, and will be adjusted for inflation from 2027.

When do payment apps send a 1099-K?

Under federal rules, when they paid you more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions in the year. Some states require lower thresholds, and card processors report with no minimum.

Do I have to report income if I didn't get a 1099?

Yes. All business income is taxable whether or not you receive a form.
