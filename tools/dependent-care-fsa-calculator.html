FSA or the child care credit?

The credit covers up to $3,000 of expenses for one child or $6,000 for two or more. Many families with income above modest levels save more with the FSA, and some can use both on different expenses. A tax professional or your tax software can compare the two for your income.

Questions

What is the dependent care FSA limit for 2026?

$7,500 per household, or $3,750 if married filing separately. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act raised it from $5,000. Employers can keep a lower plan limit.

How much does a dependent care FSA save?

Your contribution skips federal income tax, 7.65% Social Security and Medicare tax, and usually state tax. At a 22% bracket and 5% state tax, $7,500 saves about $2,600.
