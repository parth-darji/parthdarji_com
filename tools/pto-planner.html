How it picks

Every stretch of workdays between weekends and holidays is a chance to bridge. Taking 1 day next to a Monday holiday gives a 4-day weekend; taking the 4 workdays of a holiday week gives 9 days off. The planner ranks every option by days off per vacation day and fills your days from the best.

Questions

How do I get the most days off with PTO?

Take vacation days next to weekends and holidays. One day next to a Monday holiday makes a 4-day weekend, and the 4 workdays in a holiday week can give 9 days off.

What are the US federal holidays in 2027?

New Year's Day (Fri Jan 1), MLK Day (Jan 18), Presidents' Day (Feb 15), Memorial Day (May 31), Juneteenth (observed Fri Jun 18), Independence Day (observed Mon Jul 5), Labor Day (Sep 6), Columbus Day (Oct 11), Veterans Day (Nov 11), Thanksgiving (Nov 25), Christmas (observed Fri Dec 24), and New Year's Day 2028 observed Fri Dec 31, 2027.
