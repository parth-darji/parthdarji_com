What to put on an invoice

  • Your name or business and contact details, and the client's
  • A unique invoice number and the invoice date
  • Each service or product with quantity, rate and amount
  • Tax, discounts and the total due, with a due date
  • How to pay: bank details, payment link or address

Questions

How do I make an invoice for free?

Enter your details, your client's details and each item with quantity and rate, then download it as a PDF. No account is needed and nothing is uploaded.

What should an invoice include?

Your and your client's details, a unique invoice number, the date, each item with quantity, rate and amount, tax, the total due, the due date and how to pay.

Is my invoice information stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so it's there next time. It's never sent to a server. Use Start over to clear it.
