Random team generator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Paste names, pick how to split them, and shuffle. Shuffle again as many times as you like.

Teams are as even as possible: sizes differ by at most one person. Shuffling uses your browser's secure random numbers, so no name is favored.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored on a server.

Questions

How do I split a group into random teams?

Paste the names, choose the number of teams or people per team, and shuffle. Team sizes will differ by at most one.

Is the random team generator fair?

Yes. It shuffles with the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator, so every arrangement is equally likely.
