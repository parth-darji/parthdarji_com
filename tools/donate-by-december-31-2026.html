Donating by December 31, 2026: what counts

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortA gift counts for 2026 if it is made by December 31, 2026. A check counts the day you mail it, and a credit card gift counts in the year you make the charge, even if you pay the card bill in January.

When each kind of gift counts

How you giveCounts on
Check by mailThe date you mail it
Credit cardThe date of the charge
Pay-by-phone (bank)The date your bank pays it, which can be after your call
Text-message giftWhen it is charged to your phone account
Stock certificateThe date you mail or deliver it to the charity

For stock sent through your broker or the company to be moved into the charity's name, the gift counts only when the transfer is made on the company's books. Start stock gifts early in December.

New for 2026: a deduction without itemizing

Starting with 2026, people who take the standard deduction can deduct up to $1,000 of cash gifts to qualified charities, or $2,000 if married filing jointly. Gifts to donor advised funds and donated goods don't count toward it. Try the 2026 donation deduction calculator.

Get the paperwork

Keep a bank or card record for every cash gift, and get a written acknowledgment for any single gift of $250 or more. See donation receipt requirements.

Questions

Does a credit card donation on December 31 count for 2026?

Yes. A gift charged to a credit card counts in the year of the charge, even if you pay the bill in 2027.

Does a check mailed on December 31 count?

Yes. A mailed check counts on the date you mail it, as long as it clears.

When does a donation of stock count?

When you mail or deliver the certificate to the charity, or when the shares are moved into the charity's name on the company's books.
