1099 taxes in Florida

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortFlorida has no state personal income tax. As a 1099 freelancer in Florida you set aside federal taxes only: self-employment tax plus federal income tax. In the examples below that's about 20% to 27% of net profit.

What you pay in Florida

  • Federal self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare): 15.3% on 92.35% of profit. Explained here.
  • Federal income tax on profit after the standard deduction and half of the self-employment tax.
  • State income tax: none for individuals.

How much to set aside: examples

ProfitSE taxIncome taxSet aside
$40,000$5,652$2,281$7,933 (19.8%)
$75,000$10,597$6,504$17,101 (22.8%)
$120,000$16,955$15,705$32,660 (27.2%)

SE tax = self-employment tax. Single filer, 2026 federal rules, standard deduction, no other income and no business deduction beyond what's in the profit. Your real number will differ.

Many Florida freelancers simply move about 25% of every payment into a separate savings account, then true up with the 1099 tax calculator. Your deductions reduce it.

When to pay

Quarterly estimated payments to the IRS: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. Use the quarterly estimated tax calculator and pay online.

Florida business items to check

  • Sales and use tax: if you sell taxable goods or services, register with the Florida Department of Revenue to collect and send it.
  • Local business tax receipt: many Florida counties and cities ask businesses to hold one. Check with the county or city where you work.

Rules differ by business type and place. Ask a tax professional if you're unsure.

Questions

Do freelancers in Florida pay state income tax?

No. Florida has no personal income tax, so a Florida freelancer pays federal taxes only: self-employment tax and federal income tax.

How much should I set aside for taxes as a 1099 worker in Florida?

In these 2026 examples, about 20% to 27% of net profit for a single filer, depending on profit. Many people set aside around 25% and adjust.

Do I need a business license in Florida as a freelancer?

Many counties and cities require a local business tax receipt. Check with the county or city where you work.
