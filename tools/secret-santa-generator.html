Secret Santa generator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Add names, mark who shouldn't draw each other, and get a private link for each person. No sign-up, no emails collected; it all happens in your browser.

Send each person only their own link. Links are scrambled, not encrypted: someone determined could decode one, so this is for fun, not secrets. Nothing is stored on any server; draw again any time.

How it works

  1. Add everyone taking part, one name per line (at least 3).
  2. Add pairs who shouldn't draw each other, like couples or people who live together.
  3. Draw names, then copy each person's private link and send it by text or chat.

Questions

How do I draw names for Secret Santa online without signing up?

Enter everyone's names, add any pairs who shouldn't draw each other, and tap Draw names. Each person gets a private link showing only who they're buying for; no accounts or emails needed.

Can I stop couples from getting each other in Secret Santa?

Yes. Add them as a pair like 'Alex - Sam' in the can't-draw-each-other box, and the draw will avoid it in both directions.

How many people do you need for Secret Santa?

At least 3. With exclusions, you need enough people that everyone still has someone they're allowed to draw.
