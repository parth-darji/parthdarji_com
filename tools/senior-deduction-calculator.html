$6,000 senior deduction calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortFor 2025 through 2028, each person 65 or older can take an extra deduction of up to $6,000, on top of the standard or itemized deduction. It shrinks by 6% of income above $75,000 ($150,000 joint). Married couples filing separately can't take it.

Questions

Who qualifies for the $6,000 senior deduction?

People who are 65 or older by December 31 of the tax year, for 2025 through 2028, with a valid Social Security number. Married couples must file jointly.

Is the senior deduction $12,000 for couples?

Up to $6,000 for each spouse who is 65 or older, so up to $12,000 for a joint return where both qualify, before the income phase-out.

Does the senior deduction phase out?

Yes: by 6% of modified AGI above $75,000, or $150,000 for joint filers.
