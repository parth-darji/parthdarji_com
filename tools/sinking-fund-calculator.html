Questions

What is a sinking fund?

Money you save a little at a time for a known future cost, like car repairs, insurance or holidays, so you don't need a credit card when the bill comes.

How do I calculate how much to save for a sinking fund?

Subtract what you've saved from the goal and divide by the months until you need it. $1,200 due in 12 months with $200 saved is about $83 a month.
