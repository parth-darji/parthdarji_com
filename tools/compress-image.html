Questions

How do I compress an image without uploading it?

Choose your images here and tap Process images. Compression happens in your browser on your device, so the images are never sent to a server.

What is the best format to make images smaller?

WebP usually gives the smallest files for photos, then JPG. PNG is lossless and best for graphics or screenshots with sharp text.

How do I resize an image to a specific width?

Enter a max width or height in pixels; the image is scaled down to fit and keeps its proportions.
