Questions

How do I convert JPG to PDF without uploading?

Choose your images here, put them in order and tap Make PDF. The PDF is created in your browser on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Can I combine several photos into one PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as you like, reorder them, and each becomes one page of the same PDF.

Can I turn receipts into a PDF?

Yes. Photograph each receipt, add the photos here and download one PDF, useful for expense reports, FSA claims or HSA records.
