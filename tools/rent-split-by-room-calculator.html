Questions

How do you split rent fairly with different room sizes?

Split part of the rent (often 30-50%) equally for shared space, and the rest in proportion to bedroom size, adding extra for a private bathroom.

How should a couple split rent with roommates?

A common approach: the couple pays their room's share once, plus two people's shares of the common space.
