1099 taxes in Texas

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortTexas has no state personal income tax. As a 1099 freelancer in Texas you set aside federal taxes only: self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of profit) plus federal income tax. In the examples below that's about 20% to 27% of net profit.

What you pay in Texas

  • Federal self-employment tax: Social Security and Medicare on your profit. How it's calculated.
  • Federal income tax on profit after the standard deduction and half of the self-employment tax.
  • State income tax: none. There's nothing to set aside for a Texas return.

How much to set aside: examples

ProfitSE taxIncome taxSet aside
$40,000$5,652$2,281$7,933 (19.8%)
$75,000$10,597$6,504$17,101 (22.8%)
$120,000$16,955$15,705$32,660 (27.2%)

SE tax = self-employment tax. Single filer, 2026 federal rules, standard deduction, no other income and no business deduction beyond what's in the profit. Your real number will differ.

A rough rule is to put aside about a quarter of each payment, then adjust with the 1099 tax calculator. Deductions like business mileage and a home office lower the number.

When to pay

Quarterly estimated payments to the IRS: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. Work out each payment with the quarterly estimated tax calculator and pay online.

Texas business taxes to check

  • Franchise tax: the Texas Comptroller says a sole proprietorship that isn't legally organized to limit liability is not a taxable entity. A single-member LLC is, even though it files as a sole proprietor for federal tax, so it has Texas franchise tax reporting rules to check.
  • Sales tax: if you sell taxable goods or services, you may need a Texas sales tax permit.

Not sure what applies to you? Check the Comptroller's pages or ask a tax professional.

Questions

Do freelancers in Texas pay state income tax?

No. Texas has no personal income tax, so a Texas freelancer pays federal taxes only: self-employment tax and federal income tax.

How much should I set aside for taxes as a 1099 worker in Texas?

In these 2026 examples, about 20% to 27% of net profit for a single filer, depending on profit. Your own number depends on deductions, filing status and other income.

Does a Texas LLC owe franchise tax?

A single-member LLC is a taxable entity for Texas franchise tax even if it files as a sole proprietor federally. Check the Texas Comptroller's rules for what you must file.
