Tax rate: in the US, self-employment tax is 15.3% on most of your profit, plus federal and state income tax, so 25-35% total is common. Billable hours are hours clients pay for; admin, sales and invoicing usually take 25-40% of a working week. An estimate.

How it's calculated

Revenue needed = (take-home income ÷ (1 − tax rate)) + business costs. Hourly rate = revenue ÷ (billable hours × working weeks). Day rate assumes 8 billable hours.

Questions

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Add your income goal grossed up for taxes plus business costs, then divide by the hours you can actually bill in a year. $70,000 take-home at 30% tax plus $6,000 costs over 25 billable hours a week for 46 weeks is about $93 an hour.

How many billable hours does a freelancer have?

Often 20-30 hours a week, because admin, sales and invoicing take the rest of the working week.
