Questions

How do you split trip expenses fairly?

Add every expense with who paid and who it was for. Each person's share is what they owe; subtract it from what they paid. People who paid more than their share get paid back by those who paid less.

How do I figure out who owes whom?

Work out each person's balance (paid minus share), then match the people who owe with the people who are owed. This tool does that with the fewest payments.
