HSA year-end checklist for 2026

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortFor 2026 you can put up to $4,400 (self-only) or $8,750 (family) in an HSA, plus $1,000 if you are 55 or older. You have until the tax filing deadline, April 15, 2027, to add money for 2026.

1. Add up what went in this year

Count your payroll contributions, your employer's, and anything you added yourself. All of it counts toward the limit. See HSA contribution limits.

2. Top up before the deadline

If you are under the limit, you can still contribute for 2026 until April 15, 2027. Tell your HSA provider the money is for 2026. Contributions must be made in cash.

3. Over the limit? Fix it now

Extra contributions are taxed each year until removed. Ask your provider to withdraw the excess. See excess HSA contributions.

4. Turning 55?

You can add the extra $1,000 catch-up for the year. A spouse 55 or older needs their own HSA for theirs.

5. Save every 2026 medical receipt

You can pay yourself back from your HSA later, even years later, for expenses after the HSA was opened. You need the receipt to show it. See how to track HSA receipts.

6. Check what your cash is doing

Money left in the HSA's cash account may earn little. Many providers let you invest above a minimum balance. See HSA investing basics.
