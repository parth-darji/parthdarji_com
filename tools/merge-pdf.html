Merge PDF without uploading

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Private by designYour files never leave this device: everything happens inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded to any server. Once you've opened this page, it even works offline.

How it works

  1. Choose your PDFs. They're read by your browser, not sent anywhere.
  2. Put them in order with the arrows, or pick the pages to keep.
  3. Download the new PDF. No watermark, no limits on file count.

Want to check? Open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab and merge a file: you'll see no upload. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory. Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked first.

Questions

How do I merge PDF files without uploading them?

Use a tool that runs in your browser. Here, choose your PDFs, put them in order and download the merged file; the files are processed on your device and never sent to a server.

Is it safe to merge PDFs online?

Many online PDF tools upload your files to their servers. This one doesn't: everything happens in your browser, which you can confirm in your browser's Network tab.

Can I split a PDF or extract pages?

Yes. Switch to Extract or split pages, choose one PDF and enter pages like 1-3, 5, or make a separate PDF for every page.
