Invoice late fee calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Late fee

$0

Interest is simple (not compounding) and prorated by day: monthly rate × 12 ÷ 365 per day. Only charge a late fee your contract or invoice terms state up front, and check your state or country's limits; many places cap interest rates. Not legal advice.

Fair late fee practice

  • Put the late fee policy on the contract and on every invoice, before work starts.
  • 1–1.5% per month or a small flat fee are common; keep it reasonable.
  • Send a friendly reminder a few days before and after the due date first.

Questions

How do I calculate a late fee on an invoice?

For a monthly rate, multiply the invoice by the monthly rate × 12 ÷ 365 for each day overdue. A $2,500 invoice 45 days late at 1.5% a month is about $55.48.

What is a normal late fee for freelancers?

Commonly 1–1.5% per month or a small flat fee, stated in the contract and on the invoice in advance. Check local limits on interest.
