Questions

What are the 2026 IRMAA brackets?

For individuals, IRMAA starts above $109,000 of 2024 modified AGI ($218,000 joint), with Part B totals of $284.10, $405.80, $527.50, $649.20 and $689.90 per month across the brackets, per CMS.

What income is used for 2026 IRMAA?

Generally your 2024 tax return: adjusted gross income plus tax-exempt interest.

Can I appeal IRMAA?

Yes, if your income went down because of a life-changing event such as retirement or the death of a spouse. Ask Social Security to use newer income with form SSA-44.
