January 15, 2027 estimated tax payment

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortThe last estimated tax payment for 2026 is due January 15, 2027. You can skip it if you file your 2026 return by February 1, 2027 and pay everything you owe with it.

Who pays it

Freelancers, 1099 contractors, and anyone else who expects to owe $1,000 or more for 2026 after withholding and credits. See do I need to pay estimated taxes?

How much

Usually a quarter of your expected 2026 tax, adjusted for what you already paid in April, June and September. If your income in the fall was higher than planned, this is your chance to catch up. Work it out with the quarterly estimated tax calculator.

How to pay

  • Online at the IRS payments page (Direct Pay, your IRS online account, or EFTPS). Choose estimated tax, 1040-ES, tax year 2026. See how to pay online.
  • By mail with a Form 1040-ES voucher: a payment postmarked by January 15 counts as on time.

The February 1 exception

You don't have to make the January payment if you file your 2026 Form 1040 by February 1, 2027 and pay the full balance with it. Most people don't have their forms that early, so the January payment is usually easier.

Missed it?

Pay as soon as you can. The penalty is figured per quarter and grows with time. See the underpayment penalty guide.
