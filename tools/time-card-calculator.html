Converting minutes to decimal hours

Divide minutes by 60: 15 min = 0.25, 30 min = 0.5, 45 min = 0.75, 6 min = 0.1. So 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours.

Questions

How do I calculate hours worked with a lunch break?

Subtract the start time from the end time, then subtract the unpaid break. 9:00 to 5:30 with a 30-minute lunch is 8 hours.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 45/60 = 7.75 hours.

When does overtime start?

Under federal law, after 40 hours in a workweek for most hourly employees, at least 1.5 times the regular rate. Some states also have daily overtime rules.
