Questions

How much does a dog cost per year?

It depends on size, health and where you live. Food, routine vet care, insurance, grooming and boarding commonly add up to roughly $1,500-$3,000 a year, plus first-year costs. Use the calculator with your own quotes.

How much does a pet cost over its lifetime?

First-year costs plus the yearly cost times its lifespan. A pet costing $2,000 a year over 13 years is about $26,000 plus first-year extras.
