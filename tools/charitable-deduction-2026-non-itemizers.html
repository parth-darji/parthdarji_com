2026 charitable deduction if you don't itemize

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortStarting with tax year 2026, you can deduct up to $1,000 of cash gifts to qualified charities ($2,000 if married filing jointly) even if you take the standard deduction. Donated items don't count. Keep a record of every gift.

Who gets it

Only people who don't itemize, which is most taxpayers. If you itemize, you deduct charitable gifts on Schedule A as before instead.

What counts

  • Cash gifts: check, card, bank transfer or online payment.
  • To qualified charities that can receive tax-deductible gifts.
  • Made during 2026, claimed on the 2026 return you file in 2027. The law makes it permanent.

What doesn't count

  • Donated items (clothes, furniture, cars, stock): no noncash gifts.
  • Gifts to a donor advised fund, or to a supporting organization.
  • Payments to a nonprofit that aren't gifts, like a membership or ticket where you get something back.

Example

A single filer gives $40 a month to a food bank by card ($480) and $300 to a hospital charity in December: $780 in cash gifts, all deductible under the $1,000 cap. A married couple filing jointly who give $2,600 can deduct $2,000.

Records to keep

For every gift: a receipt, canceled check, bank or card record, or a note from the charity with its name, the date and the amount. For any single gift of $250 or more you also need a written acknowledgment from the charity. See donation receipt requirements.

Start now

December is when most people give. Log each gift as you make it so January isn't a search through bank statements. The free donation log builder works in your browser.

Sources

  • IRS Topic 506: Charitable contributions

Questions

Can I deduct donations if I take the standard deduction?

From tax year 2026, yes: up to $1,000 of cash gifts to qualified charities, or $2,000 if married filing jointly.

Do donated clothes or goods count for the $1,000 deduction?

No. Only cash contributions qualify. Noncash gifts can only be deducted if you itemize.

Do gifts to a donor advised fund count?

No. Cash gifts to a donor advised fund or to a supporting organization don't qualify for this deduction.
