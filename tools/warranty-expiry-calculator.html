Warranty expiry calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortEnter the date you bought something and how long the warranty lasts. You get the end date, how many days are left, and a calendar reminder so you can make a claim in time.

Warranties usually count from the purchase date or delivery date, and some start on the day the product is installed. Always check the terms that came with the product: what's covered, what voids it, and how to claim. Keep the receipt, since most warranty claims need proof of purchase.

Making a warranty claim

  1. Find the receipt or order confirmation and the model and serial number.
  2. Check the terms: many warranties cover defects, not accidents or normal wear.
  3. Contact the maker or seller before the end date, and keep a note of who you spoke to.

Where to find the date

  • The receipt or order email shows the purchase date.
  • The warranty card or the maker's website shows the length.
  • Some credit cards add extra coverage to purchases; check your card's benefits guide for the terms.

Questions

How do I calculate when a warranty ends?

Add the warranty length to the purchase date. A 12-month warranty bought on June 10 ends on June 10 of the next year. Check your terms, since some start on delivery or installation.

Do I need a receipt to make a warranty claim?

Usually yes. Most sellers and makers ask for proof of purchase showing the date, so keep the receipt or order email.

What does a reminder file (.ics) do?

It's a calendar event. Download it and open it, and it's added to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. Nothing is sent anywhere.
