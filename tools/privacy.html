Privacy and how the tools work

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortEverything you type or open in these tools stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking scripts.

What happens to your files and numbers

  • Calculators do their math in your browser. The numbers you type are never sent anywhere.
  • File tools (Merge PDF, Image to PDF, Compress images, HEIC to JPG, Remove photo metadata) open your files inside your browser and create the result on your device. Your files are never uploaded.
  • Share links (Secret Santa, Trip expense splitter) carry the information inside the link itself. Nothing is stored on a server; only people you send the link to can open it.

What is saved, and where

A few tools remember your inputs in your own browser so they're there next time: the invoice generator, holiday budget, sinking fund planner, trip splitter and your "recently used" tools. This uses your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. Clear it any time with the tool's Start over button or your browser's "clear site data".

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No ads and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, no cookies for tracking
  • No third-party scripts: the two libraries the file tools use (pdf-lib and heic2any, both MIT licensed) are hosted on this site

The website host (Cloudflare) handles normal web traffic like any server, and may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security. We don't add anything on top of that.

Check it yourself

  1. Open a file tool, then open your browser's developer tools and the Network tab.
  2. Process a file. You'll see no upload requests.
  3. Or turn on airplane mode after the page loads: the tools keep working.

Links to apps and other sites

Some pages link to Parth Darji's own apps (ShoeboxHSA, PunchMate) on the App Store. If affiliate links are ever added, they'll be clearly labeled. Embedded calculators on other websites show the same tool with a link back here.
