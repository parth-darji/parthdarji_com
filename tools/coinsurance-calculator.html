Simple in-network estimate: deductible first, then your coinsurance share, never more than your out-of-pocket max. Copays, out-of-network bills and services covered before the deductible (like preventive care) work differently.

The terms, simply

  • Allowed amount: the price your insurer agreed to, often much less than the provider's list price.
  • Deductible: what you pay first each year before insurance shares the cost.
  • Coinsurance: your percentage after the deductible, for example 20%.
  • Out-of-pocket max: the most you pay in a year for covered in-network care.

Questions

How is coinsurance calculated?

You pay the remaining deductible first, then your coinsurance percentage of the rest of the allowed amount, up to your out-of-pocket maximum. For a $3,000 bill with $1,000 deductible left and 20% coinsurance, you pay $1,000 + 20% of $2,000 = $1,400.

What happens after I reach my out-of-pocket maximum?

Your plan pays 100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the plan year.
