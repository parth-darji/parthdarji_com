Is splitting by income fair?

Many couples find it fairer than 50/50, because both people keep the same share of their income for themselves. Others split some costs 50/50 (like rent) and the rest by income. Talk it through; the numbers just make the conversation easier.

Questions

How do you split bills by income?

Add up everyone's income, find each person's percentage of the total, and pay that percentage of the shared costs. If one person earns 60% of the combined income, they pay 60% of the bills.

Should couples split rent 50/50 or by income?

It's a personal choice. Splitting by income means both people pay the same percentage of what they earn, which many couples find fairer when incomes differ.
