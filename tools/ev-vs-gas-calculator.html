How it's calculated

Questions

Is it cheaper to charge an EV than buy gas?

Usually, when you charge mostly at home. At 17 cents per kWh and 30 kWh per 100 miles, an EV costs about $5 per 100 miles, versus about $10.67 for a 30 mpg car at $3.20 a gallon. Frequent fast charging can erase much of the gap.

How much does it cost to charge an EV per year?

Miles ÷ 100 × kWh per 100 miles × your rate. 12,000 miles at 30 kWh/100 mi and 17 cents is about $612 a year at home.
