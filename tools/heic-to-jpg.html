Why HEIC?

iPhones save photos as HEIC to use less space. Some websites, Windows PCs and older apps can't open them; JPG works everywhere. On your iPhone you can switch to JPG for new photos in Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible.

Converting many large photos can take a moment on older phones, since all the work happens on your device. Photo metadata like location isn't copied into the JPG.

Questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG without uploading?

Choose your HEIC photos here and tap Convert. The conversion runs in your browser on your device, so the photos are never sent to a server.

Why can't I open HEIC files?

HEIC is Apple's space-saving photo format, and some Windows PCs, websites and older apps don't support it. Converting to JPG makes the photo open anywhere.

How do I make my iPhone take JPG photos?

Go to Settings › Camera › Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will be saved as JPG.
