1099 taxes in Nevada

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortNevada has no individual income tax. As a 1099 freelancer in Nevada you set aside federal taxes only: self-employment tax plus federal income tax, about 20% to 27% of net profit in the examples below. Nevada's own cost to know about is the State Business License.

What you pay in Nevada

  • Federal self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare): 15.3% on 92.35% of profit. How it works.
  • Federal income tax on profit after the standard deduction and half of the self-employment tax.
  • State income tax: none. The Nevada Department of Taxation says the state has no individual income tax.

The Nevada State Business License

Sole proprietors in Nevada generally need a State Business License. The Secretary of State lists the fee as $200 to register and $200 to renew, with a $100 penalty for paying late. Some businesses are exempt and receive an exemption certificate instead. Check the Secretary of State's FAQ for your case. Many Nevada cities and counties also have their own business license, so check where you work.

Commerce Tax

Nevada's Commerce Tax applies only to businesses with more than $4,000,000 in Nevada gross revenue. The Department of Taxation's FAQ says that if your revenue is $4,000,000 or less, you are not required to file a Commerce Tax return. Confirm with the Department if you're close to the line.

When to pay

Federal quarterly estimated payments: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. Use the quarterly calculator and pay online.

Questions

Do freelancers in Nevada pay state income tax?

No. The Nevada Department of Taxation says the state has no individual income tax, so a Nevada freelancer pays federal taxes only: self-employment tax and federal income tax.

Do I need a business license as a sole proprietor in Nevada?

Generally yes. The Nevada Secretary of State lists a State Business License fee of $200 to register and $200 to renew for sole proprietors, with some exemptions. Many cities and counties also have local licenses.

Does the Nevada Commerce Tax apply to freelancers?

It applies only above $4,000,000 in Nevada gross revenue. The Department of Taxation says businesses at or below that amount don't need to file a Commerce Tax return.
