Remove photo location and metadata

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Private by designYour photos never leave this device: everything happens inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded to any server. Once you've opened this page, it even works offline.

Photos from phones often hide the exact GPS location where they were taken, plus the camera model and time. Anyone you send the original file to can read it. This tool makes a clean copy without that data.

Choose one or more photos.

The clean copy is re-saved at high quality, so it looks the same but contains only the picture. Rotation is applied to the pixels so photos don't end up sideways. Many apps (like iMessage, WhatsApp) strip some data already, but email and file sharing usually keep it.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored on a server.

Questions

How do I remove location data from a photo?

Choose the photo here and tap Remove metadata. You'll get a clean copy without GPS location or other EXIF data, made on your device with no upload.

Do photos contain my location?

Phone photos often store the GPS location in hidden EXIF data if location access is on for the camera. Some apps strip it when sharing, but files sent by email or cloud links usually keep it.
