Questions

How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income?

Many freelancers set aside 25-30% of each payment, but it depends on your profit, filing status, other income and state. Self-employment tax alone is about 14.1% of profit (15.3% on 92.35% of it); income tax comes on top.

What is the self-employment tax rate for 2026?

15.3%: 12.4% for Social Security on up to $184,500 of earnings in 2026 and 2.9% for Medicare on all of it, applied to 92.35% of your net profit. An extra 0.9% Medicare tax applies above $200,000 ($250,000 joint), and you deduct half of the 15.3% when figuring income tax.

Do I pay taxes on 1099 income if I made less than $400?

You don't owe self-employment tax if your net earnings from self-employment are under $400, but the income may still be subject to income tax.

Are business expenses deducted before tax?

Yes. Self-employment tax and income tax are based on your profit: 1099 income minus ordinary and necessary business expenses.
