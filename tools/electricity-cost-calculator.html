Appliance electricity cost calculator

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Pick an appliance or type its wattage (it's on the label or charger). Use the rate from your electric bill for the most accurate answer.

Cost per month

$0

Wattages are typical examples; real use varies (fridges and AC units cycle on and off). Your rate is on your bill as cents per kWh; include delivery charges for the true cost. Time-of-use plans charge different rates by hour.

How it's calculated

kWh = watts × hours ÷ 1,000. Cost = kWh × your rate. A 1,500 W heater for 4 hours uses 6 kWh a day; at 17¢ that's $1.02 a day, about $31 a month.

Easy ways to cut the bill

  • Heat or cool the room you're in, not the whole house, and use a timer.
  • Switch remaining bulbs to LED: about 10 W instead of 60 W.
  • Run the dryer and dishwasher at off-peak hours if your plan has time-of-use rates.

Questions

How do I calculate electricity cost for an appliance?

Multiply watts by hours of use and divide by 1,000 to get kWh, then multiply by your rate. A 1,500 W heater for 4 hours uses 6 kWh; at 17 cents per kWh that's $1.02.

How much does it cost to run a space heater?

A typical 1,500 W space heater costs about 26 cents an hour at 17 cents per kWh, or about $31 a month at 4 hours a day.
