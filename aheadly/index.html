How it works

What the app does

Set aside from every payment

Federal income tax plus 15.3% self-employment tax, estimated with the current year's IRS brackets.

Safe harbor built in

Uses the smaller of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's (110% above $150,000 AGI) to help you avoid the underpayment penalty.

Questions

When are quarterly estimated taxes due?

For most people: April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15 of the next year. A date that falls on a weekend or holiday moves to the next business day.

Who needs to pay estimated taxes?

Usually anyone who expects to owe $1,000 or more when they file, for example freelancers, contractors and gig workers paid on a 1099. Check your own numbers.

Does Aheadly connect to my bank?

No. You type in what clients pay you. There is no account, no bank login and no server.

Does it include state tax?

You can add your state rate to see what to set aside. State estimated payments go to your state, separately from the IRS.

Is this tax advice?

No. Aheadly gives estimates based on IRS rates. It doesn't file or pay taxes for you. For your situation, ask a tax professional.
