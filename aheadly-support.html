How Aheadly works

Tell Aheadly your filing status, expected income and last year's tax. Each time a client pays you, tap + and add the payment. Aheadly shows how much of it to set aside and how much to pay the IRS before each quarterly due date (April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15). When you pay, tap "I paid" to log it.

Are the numbers exact?

No. They are estimates based on IRS tax brackets and self-employment tax rates for the year. Your real tax depends on deductions and other income. Aheadly isn't tax advice; check with a tax professional for your situation.

Aheadly Pro

Free: up to 10 payments. Pro adds unlimited payments, due-date reminders and CSV export. Pro is a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in Aheadly and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Cancel a subscription or get a refund

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on your iPhone go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My data

Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export a CSV first if you want a copy. Details: privacy policy.
