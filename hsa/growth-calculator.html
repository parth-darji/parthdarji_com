Questions

How much can an HSA grow?

It depends on how much you put in, how long it stays invested and the returns, which are not guaranteed. The calculator shows an estimate with a steady yearly return.

Do I have to invest my HSA?

No. Many HSA providers keep money in cash by default. Investing is optional and comes with market risk.
