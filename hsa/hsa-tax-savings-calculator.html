2026 limits: $4,400 self-only, $8,750 family, plus $1,000 if you're 55 or older. Payroll contributions also skip Social Security and Medicare tax (7.65%) up to the Social Security wage limit. California and New Jersey tax HSA contributions at the state level, so use 0% state there. This is an estimate, not tax advice.

Where the savings come from

  • Federal income tax: contributions lower your taxable income.
  • State income tax: most states follow the federal rule.
  • Payroll tax: only when the money goes in through your employer's payroll (a cafeteria plan).

Questions

How much tax does an HSA save?

Your contribution avoids federal income tax at your bracket, usually state income tax, and 7.65% payroll tax if it goes in through payroll. At a 22% bracket and 5% state tax, $4,400 through payroll saves about $1,525.

Do HSA contributions avoid FICA tax?

Yes, when they're made through your employer's payroll as pre-tax contributions. Contributions you make on your own are deducted on your tax return but still pay FICA.
