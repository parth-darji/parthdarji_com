Can I use my HSA for dental and vision?

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortYes. Most dental and eye care counts as medical care under IRS Publication 502, so your HSA can pay for it tax-free. Cosmetic work like teeth whitening doesn't qualify, and dental or vision insurance premiums generally can't be paid from an HSA.

Dental: usually eligible

  • Cleanings, exams and X-rays
  • Fillings, crowns, root canals and extractions
  • Braces and other orthodontic treatment
  • Dentures, bridges and implants
  • Gum disease treatment

Dental: usually not eligible

  • Teeth whitening and other cosmetic treatment
  • Everyday toothpaste, toothbrushes and floss

Vision: usually eligible

  • Eye exams
  • Prescription glasses, including prescription sunglasses
  • Contact lenses and contact lens solution
  • LASIK and other vision correction surgery

Vision: usually not eligible

  • Non-prescription sunglasses
  • Extended warranties or frames bought only for style with no prescription

Dental and vision insurance

Premiums for dental or vision insurance generally aren't HSA-eligible. Having a separate dental or vision plan does not stop you from contributing to an HSA, because the IRS treats it as permitted extra coverage. See HSA-eligible health plans.

Receipts to keep

Dental and eye bills are often big and spread over months, like braces or LASIK. Keep the itemized statement for each payment, plus the EOB if insurance paid part of it. These are good bills to pay yourself and reimburse later with the shoebox strategy.

Questions

Can I use my HSA for braces?

Yes. Orthodontic treatment such as braces usually qualifies as medical care under IRS Publication 502. Purely cosmetic treatment doesn't.

Can I use my HSA for glasses and contacts?

Yes. Prescription glasses, prescription sunglasses, contact lenses and solution, and eye exams usually qualify.

Can I pay dental insurance premiums with my HSA?

Generally no. HSA money can't pay most insurance premiums, including dental and vision plans.

Is LASIK HSA eligible?

Yes. LASIK and other vision correction surgery usually qualify.
