Gym, massage, therapy, weight loss: can your HSA pay?

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortThe rule from IRS Publication 502: an expense qualifies if it's mainly to diagnose, treat or prevent a specific medical condition, not just for general health. Several gray-area items qualify only with a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) from your doctor.

Item by item

Therapy and counseling Eligible

Psychotherapy, psychologists and psychiatrists for mental health treatment. Physical therapy too. Marriage counseling for general relationship help usually isn't.

Weight loss medication Depends

Prescription medicine to treat a diagnosed disease, such as obesity or diabetes, usually qualifies. Weight loss for general health or looks doesn't. Keep the prescription.

Weight loss programs Depends

Eligible when a doctor prescribes them for a specific disease such as obesity or high blood pressure. Food usually isn't.

Gym membership Needs a doctor's letter

Generally not eligible. It may qualify with an LMN for a specific condition, and only for the period the condition needs treatment.

Massage Needs a doctor's letter

Eligible only to treat a specific medical condition, with an LMN. Massage for relaxation doesn't qualify.

Vitamins and supplements Needs a doctor's letter

Generally not eligible, unless a doctor recommends them to treat a specific condition (for example, iron for diagnosed anemia).

Botox Depends

Eligible for medical treatment, such as chronic migraine or excessive sweating. Cosmetic Botox isn't.

Mattress Needs a doctor's letter

Only the extra cost of a mattress a doctor prescribes for a condition, with an LMN. A regular mattress isn't eligible.

How a Letter of Medical Necessity works

Your doctor writes that you have a specific condition and that the item or service is needed to treat it, and for how long. Keep the letter with the receipts. Many HSA providers have a template form.

Questions

Can I use my HSA for a gym membership?

Generally no. It may qualify only with a Letter of Medical Necessity from a doctor for a specific medical condition.

Can I use my HSA for weight loss injections?

Prescription weight loss medicine used to treat a diagnosed disease, such as obesity, usually qualifies. Use for general health or appearance doesn't.

Can I use my HSA for therapy?

Yes. Mental health therapy with a licensed provider and physical therapy usually qualify.

Can I use my HSA for massage?

Only with a Letter of Medical Necessity for a specific medical condition. Massage for relaxation doesn't qualify.
