Which receipts to keep for your HSA

By Parth Darji · Updated November 22, 2026

In shortKeep an itemized receipt or statement that shows who provided the care, when, what it was for, and how much you paid. A card slip on its own isn't enough. Clear photos or PDFs are fine.

What a good HSA receipt shows

DetailWhy it matters
Provider or store nameShows who you paid.
Date of serviceProves the expense came after your HSA was opened.
What it was forShows it's a qualified medical expense (an itemized list, not just "Total").
Amount you paidThe part you paid yourself, after insurance.
Patient name (if shown)Shows it was for you, your spouse or a dependent.

Documents worth keeping

  • Itemized receipts from doctors, dentists, eye care, labs and pharmacies.
  • Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your insurer. It shows what insurance paid and your share ("patient responsibility"). How to read an EOB.
  • Provider statements or bills plus proof you paid them, such as a card or bank statement line.
  • Prescription receipts, and for over-the-counter medicine, the store receipt listing the item. Since 2020 (CARES Act), many over-the-counter medicines and menstrual products qualify without a prescription.
  • Letters of Medical Necessity for items that only qualify with a doctor's letter, such as some massage therapy or gym programs.

Not enough on its own

  • A credit card slip that shows only a total.
  • A bank statement line without an itemized receipt.
  • A pharmacy receipt where medical and non-medical items are mixed and not labeled.

How long to keep them

Keep each receipt for as long as you might reimburse yourself for it, and then at least three more years after you file the tax return for the year you took the money out (the usual period the IRS has to review a return). With the shoebox strategy, that can mean decades.

Paper or digital?

The IRS accepts electronic records if they are accurate and readable. Many receipts are printed on thermal paper that fades within a few years, so photograph or scan them soon. Keep a backup copy somewhere other than one phone. Already lost one? How to get proof again.

A simple system

  1. Pay the bill and ask for an itemized receipt.
  2. Photograph it the same day.
  3. Note the date, provider, amount, category and patient.
  4. Mark it "not reimbursed" until you pay yourself back, then record the withdrawal date.

Questions

Is a credit card statement enough proof for an HSA withdrawal?

Usually not on its own, because it doesn't show what you bought. Keep the itemized receipt or an EOB, and the statement as proof of payment.

Can I keep HSA receipts as photos?

Yes. The IRS accepts electronic copies that are accurate and readable. Keep a backup.

How long should I keep HSA receipts?

Until you reimburse yourself for them, and then at least three years after filing the tax return for that year.
