Money you take out for qualified medical expenses is tax-free. For anything else, you pay income tax, plus a 20% additional tax if you're under 65. Check what a withdrawal would cost.

How to make the same withdrawal tax-free

If you've paid medical bills yourself, even years ago, after your HSA was opened, you can reimburse yourself for them tax-free. That's why keeping receipts matters: see the shoebox strategy and how to reimburse yourself.

Took money out by mistake?

Many HSA providers let you return a mistaken distribution by the tax deadline for that year, if it was a true mistake (for example, you thought an expense qualified). Ask your provider for their "mistaken distribution" form.

Questions

What is the penalty for HSA withdrawals for non-medical expenses?

The withdrawal is added to your taxable income, plus a 20% additional tax if you're under 65 and not disabled.

Can I withdraw from my HSA after 65 without penalty?

Yes. After 65 there's no 20% penalty. Non-medical withdrawals are taxed as income, like a traditional IRA, and medical withdrawals stay tax-free.

Can I take money out of my HSA for past medical bills?

Yes, for qualified expenses you had after your HSA was opened, as long as you didn't already pay them with HSA money or deduct them. Keep the receipts.
