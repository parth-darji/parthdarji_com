Can I pay insurance premiums with my HSA?

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

In shortUsually not. But there are four exceptions: COBRA, health coverage while you get unemployment benefits, qualified long-term care insurance, and Medicare and some employer retiree coverage once you're 65.

The four exceptions

  1. COBRA. Premiums for continued coverage after leaving a job.
  2. Coverage while on unemployment. Health insurance premiums while you're receiving federal or state unemployment compensation.
  3. Long-term care insurance. Qualified policies, up to a yearly limit that depends on your age.
  4. At 65 and older: Medicare Part A, B and D premiums, Medicare Advantage, and your share of employer health or retiree coverage. Medigap (Medicare supplement) premiums are not eligible.

Not eligible

  • Regular health insurance premiums while you're working, including marketplace plans
  • Dental and vision insurance premiums
  • Medigap premiums

More on retirement: your HSA after 65. Dental and vision care itself does qualify: see what counts.

Keep proof

Save the premium statement and proof of payment like any other HSA receipt, along with proof of the exception (like your COBRA notice or unemployment record).

Questions

Can I use my HSA for COBRA premiums?

Yes. COBRA continuation coverage premiums are one of the exceptions.

Can I use my HSA to pay Medicare premiums?

Yes, once you're 65: Medicare Part A, B and D and Medicare Advantage premiums qualify. Medigap premiums don't.

Can I use my HSA for health insurance premiums?

Generally no, except COBRA, coverage while receiving unemployment benefits, long-term care insurance, and certain coverage after 65.
