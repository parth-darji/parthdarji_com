Questions

Is an HSA plan cheaper than a PPO?

Often in years with low or normal medical bills, because premiums are lower and HSA money comes with tax savings and sometimes employer contributions. In years with big bills, a PPO can cost less. Compare your plans' numbers.

What counts as the cost of an HSA plan?

Premiums plus out-of-pocket bills, minus your employer's HSA contribution and the tax you save on your own HSA contributions.
