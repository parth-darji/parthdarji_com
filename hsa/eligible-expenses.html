Is it HSA eligible?

By Parth Darji · Updated November 23, 2026

Search 35 common expenses and see whether they usually qualify for tax-free HSA money under IRS Publication 502. Your HSA provider may have its own list; when in doubt, ask them or a tax professional.

35 expenses

Acupuncture Eligible

Fully eligible medical care performed by licensed practitioners under IRS Pub 502.

Air Purifier / HEPA Filter Needs a doctor's letter

Requires physician Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) specifying severe asthma or respiratory disease.

Allergy & Sinus Medication Eligible

Eligible without a prescription under the CARES Act (Claritin, Zyrtec, Flonase).

Ambulance Transport Eligible

Ground, water, or air ambulance services for emergency medical care.

Bandages & First Aid Kits Eligible

Band-aids, sterile dressings, elastic wraps, and burn treatment supplies.

Blood Pressure Monitor Eligible

Diagnostic home monitoring cuffs and automated digital pulse monitors.

Blood Tests & Lab Panels Eligible

Quest, LabCorp, metabolic blood panels, lipid screens, and biopsy tests.

Braces & Orthodontics Eligible

Comprehensive orthodontic care: metal braces, clear aligners, retainers, adjustments.

Breast Pump & Supplies Eligible

Electric or manual pumps, milk storage bags, tubing, and lactation accessories.

Chiropractic Adjustments Eligible

Spinal adjustments and therapy performed by licensed chiropractors.

Contact Lenses & Solutions Eligible

Prescription contacts, multi-purpose disinfectant saline, and storage cases.

Cosmetic Surgery & Botox Not eligible

Elective procedures purely for appearance enhancement are strictly excluded under IRC § 213(d).

Dental Cleanings & Exams Eligible

Preventative dental cleanings, fluoride treatments, sealants, and cavity fillings.

Dental Implants & Crowns Eligible

Surgical tooth implants, abutments, porcelain crowns, and root canals.

Doctor Copays & Coinsurance Eligible

Primary care, urgent care, pediatrician, and specialist consultation fees.

Eye Exams & Prescription Glasses Eligible

Comprehensive optical exams, frames, lenses, anti-glare coatings, Rx sunglasses.

Flu Shots & Vaccinations Eligible

Annual influenza vaccines, shingles, hepatitis, COVID-19, and travel immunizations.

Gym Membership / Fitness Classes Needs a doctor's letter

Ineligible for general wellbeing; requires doctor LMN diagnosing a specific medical condition (e.g., cardiac rehab, obesity).

Hearing Aids & Batteries Eligible

Audiology exams, digital hearing aids, replacement button batteries, and repairs.

Hospital Care & Inpatient Surgery Eligible

Inpatient room and board, nursing care, operating room fees, and anesthesia.

Insulin & Diabetic Supplies Eligible

Injectable insulin, continuous glucose monitors (CGM), test strips, lancets.

LASIK & PRK Vision Surgery Eligible

Laser eye surgery to correct refractive errors, cataract extraction, corneal lens implants.

Massage Therapy Needs a doctor's letter

Requires doctor prescription or LMN explicitly diagnosing physical trauma, scoliosis, or nerve pain.

Maternity Clothing Not eligible

General apparel is an ordinary personal expense, not a deductible medical necessity.

Mental Health Counseling Eligible

Licensed therapist, psychologist, clinical psychiatrist, and marriage/family counseling.

OTC Pain Relievers Eligible

Advil (ibuprofen), Tylenol (acetaminophen), Aleve (naproxen), aspirin under CARES Act.

Orthopedic Mattress / Pillows Needs a doctor's letter

Requires physician letter proving standard bedding aggravates a diagnosed spinal pathology.

Physical Therapy & Rehab Eligible

Post-surgery recovery, physical therapy sessions, orthopedic rehabilitation.

Prescription Medications Eligible

All FDA-approved drugs prescribed by licensed physicians for medical treatment.

Sunscreen (SPF 15+ Broad Spectrum) Eligible

Broad-spectrum sunscreen lotions and sprays formulated to prevent sun-induced skin cancer.

Teeth Whitening & Veneers Not eligible

Cosmetic dental bleaching and elective cosmetic veneers are excluded under IRS Pub 502.

Toiletries & Deodorant Not eligible

General personal hygiene products (toothpaste, shampoo, deodorant) do not qualify.

Vasectomy & Reversals Eligible

Voluntary sterilization surgery and vasectomy reversal procedures.

Weight Loss Clinic & Program Needs a doctor's letter

Requires physician diagnosis of morbid obesity, hypertension, or diabetes treated by the program.

X-Rays, CT Scans & MRI Eligible

Diagnostic radiography, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), and computed tomography.

What the labels mean

  • Eligible usually qualifies when it's for medical care for you, your spouse or a dependent.
  • Needs a doctor's letter qualifies only with a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN) from a doctor explaining the medical reason.
  • Not eligible is generally a personal or cosmetic expense.

Questions

Are over-the-counter medicines HSA eligible?

Many are. Since the 2020 CARES Act, over-the-counter medicines such as pain relievers and allergy medicine, and menstrual products, qualify without a prescription.

Is a gym membership HSA eligible?

Generally no, unless a doctor writes a Letter of Medical Necessity for a specific medical condition.

Are glasses and contact lenses HSA eligible?

Yes. Prescription glasses, contact lenses, solution and eye exams usually qualify.
