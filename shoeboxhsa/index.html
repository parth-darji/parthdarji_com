Why keep every receipt?

The IRS sets no deadline for paying yourself back from an HSA. If you pay a medical bill out of pocket today and leave the money invested, you can withdraw the same amount tax-free years later. The catch: you need the receipt. ShoeboxHSA is built for exactly that.

  1. Pay the bill with your own money, not your HSA card.
  2. Snap the receipt. The app reads the amount, provider and date for you to check.
  3. Let your HSA grow while the receipt waits safely in the app.
  4. Reimburse yourself whenever you want, and mark the receipt as paid back with the withdrawal date.

Questions

Is there a time limit to reimburse myself from my HSA?

No. IRS Publication 969 sets no deadline. The expense must have happened after your HSA was opened, and you need records that prove it. How reimbursing yourself works.

Which receipts should I keep?

An itemized receipt or statement showing the date of service, the provider, what it was for and what you paid. The full checklist.

Does the app need an account or upload my receipts?

No. There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What does it cost?

It's free to download and try. An optional Pro upgrade unlocks more; current prices are shown in the App Store.

Is this tax advice?

No. ShoeboxHSA is a record-keeping tool. For questions about your own taxes, ask a tax professional.
